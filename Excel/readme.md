## Business Problem
The business needed a centralized view of sales and profit performance across regions and product categories to better understand which segments were driving revenue, which areas were underperforming, and where future growth opportunities existed.

## Key Insights
- Generated approximately $9.6M in total sales and $1.6M in total profit.


- Identified significant differences in sales and profitability across regions.


- Revealed top-performing product categories contributing the highest revenue.


- Highlighted category-level performance trends that can support future business planning.


- Enabled year-over-year performance comparisons through interactive dashboard filtering.



## Recommendations
- Increase investment in high-performing regions and categories.

- Investigate underperforming segments to identify improvement opportunities.

- Monitor profitability alongside sales to ensure sustainable growth.


- Use category-level insights to optimize product portfolio decisions.


- Continue tracking performance trends to support future forecasting and planning.
