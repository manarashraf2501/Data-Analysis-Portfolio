## Business Problem

The company needed visibility into supply chain performance to understand how sales, delivery delays, and product returns were affecting revenue and customer satisfaction.

## Key Findings


- Generated approximately $40.8M net revenue from 10K orders.

  
- Revenue fluctuated throughout the year, with March and October representing peak sales periods.

- Return rate reached 9.9%, indicating a significant impact on realized revenue.


- Nearly 48K units were returned.


- Certain products consistently appeared among the highest-returned items.

- Approximately 60% of delayed orders were concentrated in specific regions.


- The West region recorded the highest number of delayed deliveries.


- Several products showed recurring delivery issues.


- Hydrating Moisturizer emerged as a top-performing product.


- Product categories contributed differently to both revenue generation and return behavior.


## Recommendations
- Investigate root causes of delays in the West region to improve delivery performance.


- Review inventory and quality control processes for high-return products.


- Align stock planning with seasonal demand peaks identified in revenue trends.


- Monitor return-prone categories to reduce reverse logistics costs.


- Focus marketing and inventory investments on high-performing products with lower return rates.
